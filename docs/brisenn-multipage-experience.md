# BRISÉNN multipage experience

The Next.js static export now owns `/Web-Demos/brisenn/`. GitHub Pages copies the existing photographic, film and material assets before overlaying the new export. Product URLs remain `/brisenn/products/<handle>/`.

## Pages

- Home: opening room film, complete 20-second 720p film with its soundtrack, scroll-driven connection footage, before-and-after and three paths into the site.
- Products: complete 125-product catalogue; each product retains its own detail page, exact variants, original photography and interactive material viewer.
- Collections: six distinct routes following the original store's product families.
- Compose: 36 colours, two finishes, individual-link selection, 2–24 links, physical acrylic material study, room/detail/full views, touch rotation and pinch, exact pricing, save and share.
- In your space: current composition, private local photograph upload, placement, before/after, optional two-point measurement and PNG export.
- Custom, How it works, Size guide and About: independent pages based on the original studio's structure and published information.

The original `#studio` and `#room` bookmarks redirect to the dedicated pages. Composition state passes through a validated share URL and session storage. No server or customer uploads are required. Original-store links handle purchase and custom inquiries.

## Official information

Navigation and editorial facts were checked against brisenn.com and its Custom, How it works, Size guide and About pages. The original studio names are Bryan and Senyo. The size formula is 16.5 cm for the first link plus 11.5 cm per subsequent link. Product source inconsistencies remain disclosed in the catalogue audit and individual product pages.

## Verification

TypeScript and catalogue integrity checks run before the static build. Browser verification covers every product export, the complete collection, product variants and 3D viewers, plus the multipage visitor journeys, film/audio, mobile assembly, composition editing, save/share, room placement/calibration/export, navigation and Safari responsive layouts. Desktop and mobile screenshots are retained in CI artifacts for visual inspection.
