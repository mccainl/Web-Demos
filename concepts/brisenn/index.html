THE INFINITY COLLECTION

Blue Fade.

Sixteen handmade acrylic links. A considered movement from midnight blue to clear, with polished and matte surfaces that change with the light.

€379.99EUR
Compose your pieceView at BRISÉNN
Details

16 acrylic links, approximately 189 cm as a straight chain. Two of each colour and finish: Midnight Blue, Dark Blue, Blue and Clear.

Material & making

Designed and handmade in Amsterdam. Polished acrylic catches and carries the light; a matte finish gives a softer, frosted effect.

Dimensions & placement

Each link measures approximately 16.5 by 9 cm. Composition length varies with the number of links and the way they are hung. Confirm mounting suitability with BRISÉNN.
